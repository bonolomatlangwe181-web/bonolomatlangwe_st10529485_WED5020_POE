About Her Fitness

Her Fitness was born from a simple idea: activewear should make you feel as good as it looks. We design performance pieces that move with you — from the yoga studio to the tennis court and everywhere in between.

Based in South Africa, we are proud to offer locally focused service with WhatsApp ordering, fast delivery across Johannesburg and Pretoria, and a 7-day return policy that takes the stress out of online shopping.

Every piece in our collection is chosen with care, focusing on comfort, durability and style. We believe that when you feel confident in what you wear, you perform better in everything you do.
